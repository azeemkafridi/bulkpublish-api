# Influencer Campaign Planner

Create an execution-ready creator campaign brief without assuming access to creator accounts or promising outreach.

## Include

- Campaign objective, audience, creator criteria, brief, deliverables, review milestones, usage rights, disclosure language, and success metrics.
- Platform-specific post formats and media requirements from `platform-reference`.
- A content handoff that can become BulkPublish drafts or scheduled posts on the user's connected channels.
- Approval checkpoints before paid or sponsored content is published.
- Measurement mapping using `get-analytics`, including which metrics BulkPublish can and cannot report.

Treat creator selection, contracting, payment, and account access as separate actions requiring the user's direction.
