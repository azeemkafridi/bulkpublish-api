# Social Media Content Skills

Reusable skills for planning, adapting, reviewing, scheduling, and publishing social media content from AI agents through BulkPublish.

## Skills

### Content creation and planning

- [`social-content-strategy`](social-content-strategy/SKILL.md) — Build an audience-aware content strategy from a goal, offer, or campaign brief.
- [`platform-content-adapter`](platform-content-adapter/SKILL.md) — Adapt one message to each target platform while preserving the core idea.
- [`social-campaign-builder`](social-campaign-builder/SKILL.md) — Turn a campaign brief into a coordinated multi-post package.
- [`social-media-calendar`](social-media-calendar/SKILL.md) — Plan and schedule a practical cross-platform publishing calendar.
- [`media-preflight`](media-preflight/SKILL.md) — Check media and post-format requirements before upload or publishing.
- [`social-post-review`](social-post-review/SKILL.md) — Review drafts for quality, platform fit, and publishing risks.
- [`hashtag-keyword-research`](hashtag-keyword-research/SKILL.md) — Research relevant keywords and hashtag sets for BulkPublish content.
- [`social-trend-research`](social-trend-research/SKILL.md) — Turn verified trends into timely content opportunities.
- [`brand-voice-editor`](brand-voice-editor/SKILL.md) — Edit content into a consistent, platform-ready brand voice.
- [`social-copy-ab-testing`](social-copy-ab-testing/SKILL.md) — Design measurable copy experiments and BulkPublish variants.
- [`content-repurposing`](content-repurposing/SKILL.md) — Repurpose source content into a cross-platform social package.
- [`social-localization`](social-localization/SKILL.md) — Localize content for language, region, culture, and platform.
- [`influencer-campaign-planner`](influencer-campaign-planner/SKILL.md) — Plan creator campaigns and BulkPublish handoffs.
- [`ugc-content-planner`](ugc-content-planner/SKILL.md) — Plan permissioned UGC collection, moderation, and publication.
- [`rss-to-social`](rss-to-social/SKILL.md) — Configure controlled RSS-to-social workflows with BulkPublish.
- [`link-tracking-review`](link-tracking-review/SKILL.md) — Review BulkPublish link tracking and click measurement.
- [`campaign-performance-report`](campaign-performance-report/SKILL.md) — Report campaign results using BulkPublish analytics.
- [`social-content-audit`](social-content-audit/SKILL.md) — Audit content quality, platform fit, and BulkPublish readiness.
