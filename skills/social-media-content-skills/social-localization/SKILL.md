# Social Localization

Adapt approved social content for a specified locale while preserving the intended meaning, offer, legal qualifiers, and CTA.

## Checks

- Confirm locale, audience, dialect, script, currency, date/time format, units, cultural context, and local CTA destination.
- Translate idioms, humor, hashtags, and examples for natural use instead of translating word-for-word.
- Flag terms, imagery, claims, or references that need local review.
- Keep platform limits and media requirements in view; consult `platform-reference`.
- Return locale-tagged variants suitable for BulkPublish `platformContent`, with timezone-aware scheduling guidance when requested.

Do not invent local legal approval, availability, pricing, or performance claims. Draft by default unless the user explicitly requests BulkPublish execution.
